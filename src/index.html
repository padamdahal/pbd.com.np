Top Trusted News Sites & Tech Media Portals in Nepal

Nepal’s digital media landscape has expanded rapidly over the past decade, giving readers instant access to thousands of online portals across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Biratnagar, and the global Nepali diaspora. While speed and reach have improved dramatically, separating accountable journalism from viral rumor mills requires knowing which trusted news sites of Nepal consistently uphold editorial verification, transparent ownership, and Press Council Nepal standards.

How to Evaluate Reliable Nepali Online News Portals

Before sharing a breaking headline or citing market figures, look for four baseline signals of newsroom credibility:

  • Named Masthead & Newsroom Accountability: Credible Nepali publications clearly list their editor-in-chief, publisher, Kathmandu or provincial office address, and Department of Information and Broadcasting registration.
  • Primary-Source Attribution: Dependable reporters cite named officials, parliamentary records, Nepal Rastra Bank circulars, or audited filings rather than anonymous screenshots circulating on social feeds.
  • Clear Separation of Reporting and Opinion: Established dailies distinguish straight news dispatches from op-eds, sponsored advertorials, and political commentary.
  • Consistent Correction Standards: Reputable newsrooms update developing stories transparently rather than quietly deleting inaccurate claims.

Leading English & Nepali Newsrooms

For English-language readers, policy researchers, and international observers, The Kathmandu Post, The Himalayan Times, and myRepublica form the backbone of daily broadsheet reporting, complemented by Nepali Times for long-form investigative journalism on climate, labor migration, and governance. In Nepali-language digital reporting, Online Khabar, Setopati, ekantipur, and Nagarik News maintain extensive provincial correspondent networks alongside dedicated bilingual desks, while BBC News Nepali brings independent fact-checking and global context in Devanagari.

Following Tech News in Nepal & Capital Markets

As Nepal’s software export sector, FinTech wallets, EV adoption, and broadband infrastructure accelerate, specialized tech news Nepal publications have become indispensable. TechPana leads real-time Nepali and English coverage of Nepal Telecommunications Authority (NTA) regulations, cybersecurity advisories, AI policy, and digital literacy. ICT Frame tracks startup ecosystems, enterprise software, and IT policy debates, while GadgetByte Nepal delivers independent hardware reviews and verified local smartphone and laptop pricing. For investors tracking NEPSE equities, IPO allotments, and commercial bank quarterly disclosures, Sharesansar and MeroLagani remain the primary market data and financial news desks.
