Powered By Digital · Editorial Desk & Mission

About Powered By Digital

An independent Nepali digital publishing desk curating live headlines from Nepal’s established newsrooms, ICT publications, and financial portals in one fast, distraction-free reader.

Empowering people by digital means

Our Mission

Powered By Digital (pbd.com.np) exists to help readers across Nepal and the global Nepali diaspora follow credible national journalism and technology reporting without sifting through social media clutter or clickbait mirrors.

Our homepage brings together two complementary tools: a real-time Nepal News & Tech Pulse aggregating public headlines from leading English and Nepali newsrooms, and a curated Trusted Media Directory highlighting verified broadsheets, digital dailies, ICT journalists, and capital-market desks.

Editorial & Verification Standards

We do not republish full articles or strip attribution from journalists. Every headline and directory card links directly to the original publisher so readership, subscriptions, and credit stay with the reporting newsroom.

Before an outlet is added to the Powered By Digital directory, we review four baseline standards:

  • Transparent Masthead: Clearly published editor-in-chief, publisher entity, newsroom address, and Press Council Nepal or Department of Information and Broadcasting accountability.
  • Original Reporting: A demonstrable record of on-the-ground national, provincial, technology, or market reporting rather than automated scraping.
  • Source Attribution: Consistent citation of named officials, public circulars, corporate filings, and primary documents.
  • Clear Labeling: Distinct separation between news dispatches, opinion columns, and sponsored placements.

Fast, Account-Free Reading Experience

Powered By Digital is engineered as a lightweight static web application with zero login walls. Readers can filter headlines by topic or newsroom, search across titles and summaries in English or Devanagari, switch between Light and Dark reading modes, and save stories to a local Read Later list stored strictly on their own device.

Sister Platform: Nepali Web Tools

Long-time visitors may remember Powered By Digital for our free browser-based utilities—including Nepali Typing, Preeti ↔ Unicode conversion, BS ↔ AD Date Converter, Nepali Dictionary, and Nepal Income Tax Calculator. To give both news readers and utility users a faster, cleaner experience, all 20 utilities have permanently moved to our dedicated sister platform at nepaliwebtools.link, with permanent redirects in place so existing bookmarks keep working seamlessly.

Corrections & Publisher Inquiries

We regularly audit listed newsroom links, RSS feeds, and editorial descriptions to keep the directory accurate. If you represent an established Nepali newsroom or technology publication with a public RSS/Atom feed—or if you notice a broken link or outdated description—please review our Privacy Policy or check back on pbd.com.np for updates.
