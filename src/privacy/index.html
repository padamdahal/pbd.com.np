Privacy Policy

Clear, plain-language explanation of how Powered By Digital (pbd.com.np) handles local browser preferences, headline fetching, third-party publisher links, and Google AdSense advertising.

Last updated: November 2026

1. No Accounts & No Personal Data Collection

Powered By Digital (https://pbd.com.np/) does not require user registration, login accounts, newsletters, or contact forms to browse headlines or use our media directory. We do not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or reading history on our servers.

2. Local Browser Storage

To provide a responsive reading experience without tracking you across servers, our site stores a small set of preferences strictly inside your browser:

  • Theme Preference (pbd_theme): Saved in localStorage so your chosen Light or Dark mode persists between visits.
  • Saved to Read Later (pbd_saved_articles): Bookmarked headlines are stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. You can remove individual items or clear the entire list at any time from the Saved drawer or your browser settings.
  • Headline Feed Cache (pbd_feed_cache_v1): A temporary 10-minute copy of public RSS headlines stored in sessionStorage to speed up navigation and reduce repeated network requests.

None of these browser storage entries are uploaded to Powered By Digital or shared with other users.

3. Live Headline Fetching & Edge Infrastructure

When the homepage loads live headlines from /api/feed, our edge function fetches publicly available RSS and Atom feeds from Nepali news and technology publishers and returns a cached JSON summary. Search queries and category filters run entirely inside your browser—we do not log what headlines you search for or bookmark.

Like standard websites hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure, basic network metadata (such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL) may be processed temporarily at the network edge to protect against abuse, DDoS traffic, and service outages.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Powered By Digital displays advertisements served by Google AdSense to keep this service free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or similar identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to pbd.com.np and other websites across the internet.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads tailored to your visits to this site and/or other sites on the web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings ↗.
  • You can review how Google uses data from partner sites at Google Privacy & Terms ↗.

5. External News Portals & Sister Websites

Every headline and outlet listing on Powered By Digital links directly to external publishers (such as Nepali and English newsrooms) or to our sister utility portal at nepaliwebtools.link. Once you leave pbd.com.np, your visit is governed by the privacy policy and cookie practices of that destination website.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically if site features, hosting providers, or advertising configurations change. Material updates will be reflected on this page alongside a revised “Last updated” date. For more context about who operates this site, visit our About page.
