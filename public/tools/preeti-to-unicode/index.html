Practical Guide: Bidirectional Preeti & Unicode Workflows in Nepal

Whether you are migrating legacy government Word files to Unicode or preparing modern Unicode drafts for traditional printing presses, here is how both directions fit real workflows in Nepal.

Part A — Preeti to Unicode (Modernizing Legacy Files)

Preeti encodes Nepali shapes inside English ASCII slots, so text like g]kfn renders as nonsense on Android, iOS, macOS, and web browsers without PREETI.TTF installed.

Convert legacy .docx notices, legal deeds, and office reports to standard UTF-8 Unicode before uploading to Lok Sewa Aayog, Nagarik App, Supreme Court portals, websites, or social media so your text stays searchable and readable everywhere.

Part B — Unicode to Preeti (Printing Press & Legacy DTP)

Many offset printing presses, local municipalities, and DTP studios in Nepal still rely on older Adobe PageMaker, CorelDRAW, or legacy Microsoft Word templates whose shaping engines split or break Unicode Devanagari conjuncts.

Switch to Unicode → Preeti (or share via ?mode=unicode-to-preeti), convert your finalized Unicode draft into clean Preeti ASCII, paste it into PageMaker/CorelDRAW, and apply the Preeti font.

Part C — Fixing Common Edge Cases (Ikar ि & Reph र्)

In Preeti, Chhoti Ikar (l) sits before the consonant cluster (lIf → क्षि) and Reph ({) sits after the syllable (sd{ → कर्म). Unicode stores them in exact phonetic order.

Keep Smart Cleanup enabled to strip stray spaces after halantas (्), and quickly scan rare triple-conjuncts or manually typed Alt-code glyphs before sending final documents to print.

Frequently Asked Questions (प्रिती ↔ युनिकोड)

Answers to common questions about Preeti font, bidirectional Unicode conversion, Kantipur compatibility, printing press workflows, and privacy.

What is Preeti font? / प्रिती फन्ट के हो?

Preeti (प्रिती) is Nepal's most widely used legacy TrueType font that remaps standard English ASCII keys to Devanagari glyphs (for example, typing g]kfn renders नेपाल). Because it is not Unicode-encoded, Preeti text appears as garbled English characters on smartphones, browsers, and government portals unless converted to standard UTF-8 Nepali Unicode.

How do I convert Preeti to Unicode? / प्रितीबाट युनिकोड कसरी रूपान्तरण गर्ने?

Keep the Preeti → Unicode tab selected, paste your Preeti text or upload a .txt file in the left box, and clean Nepali Unicode appears instantly on the right. Click Copy Output or Download .txt to paste the result into Lok Sewa Aayog, Nagarik App, Microsoft Word, WhatsApp, or Facebook.

How do I convert Unicode back to Preeti for printing, PageMaker, or CorelDRAW? / युनिकोडबाट प्रितीमा कसरी बदल्ने?

Switch to the Unicode → Preeti tab (or click the Swap ⇄ button) and paste your Devanagari Unicode text. The converter automatically reorders Chhoti Ikar (ि → l) and Reph (र् → {) and maps conjuncts like क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ, श्र, क्र, प्र, and द्ध into authentic Preeti keystrokes ready for Adobe PageMaker, CorelDRAW, and offset printing templates.

Does this converter work with Kantipur, Fontasy Himali, and PCS Nepali fonts?

Yes. Kantipur, Fontasy Himali, Sagarmatha, and PCS Nepali share nearly the same legacy keyboard layout as Preeti. Almost all body text converts cleanly in one click, though rare decorative symbols or custom Alt-code ligatures in non-Preeti fonts may need a quick visual check.

How are Nepali numbers (१२३) and punctuation (।) converted between Preeti and Unicode?

In Preeti font, Nepali digits ० through ९ are typed with the shifted number row keys ) ! @ # $ % ^ & * (, and the Nepali full stop Purnabiram (।) is typed with the period key (.). This converter maps all ten Nepali digits and punctuation marks accurately in both directions.

Why do Chhoti Ikar (ि) and Reph (र्) sometimes need proofreading in complex conjuncts?

Preeti stores Chhoti Ikar visually before a consonant (lj) and Reph visually after a syllable (sd{), whereas Unicode stores them logically after the base consonant (वि) and before the base syllable (कर्म). Our engine automatically reorders both across full conjunct clusters, though manually mis-typed legacy files with stray spaces or broken halantas should be proofread quickly.

Does this Preeti ↔ Unicode converter work offline without internet?

Yes. All conversion rules, reordering logic, file upload, and .txt download run 100% inside your browser with zero external script dependencies. Once loaded, you can continue converting Preeti and Unicode documents completely offline.

Is my text private when converting official notices or personal documents?

Yes. Your text is processed strictly on your own device and is never sent to any server, API, or database. Even switching conversion directions only updates the ?mode= URL parameter locally in your browser.
