    Frequently asked questions

    Where can I find EV charging stations in Nepal?

    This map lists public charging stations tagged on OpenStreetMap. Use Near me or search a city.

    Is every charger in Nepal on this map?

    No. Only mapped public stations appear. Confirm opening hours and plug type before a long trip.

    Does this need an account?
