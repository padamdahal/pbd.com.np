Vehicle tax in Nepal is determined based on engine displacement (CC) or motor output (kW for EVs) under each provincial finance act. Calculate your total payable amount including annual road tax, bluebook renewal fees, third-party insurance, and late fines directly before visiting the Transport Management Office (Yatayat).

Vehicle tax calculator – frequently asked questions

How is vehicle tax calculated in Nepal?

Vehicle tax in Nepal is determined by your province, vehicle type (two-wheeler or four-wheeler), and engine displacement capacity measured in CC or kW for electric vehicles.

What fine is charged for late bluebook renewal?

Late payment penalties depend on the province and duration of delay, starting from 5% for early delays and scaling up to 20% or 30% for delays exceeding a year.

Are EV (Electric Vehicle) tax rates different?

Yes, electric motorcycles, scooters, and cars have separate tax slabs based on motor power output (kW) instead of engine CC.

Does vehicle tax vary by province in Nepal?

Yes. Each provincial government (Bagmati, Gandaki, Koshi, etc.) sets its own annual vehicle tax rates and renewal penalty structure.

What additional charges are added to the annual tax?

In addition to road tax, bluebook renewal fees, third-party insurance, and route/fitness permits (for commercial vehicles) may apply.

Where can I pay my vehicle tax in Nepal?

You can pay directly at the Transport Management Office (Yatayat) or online through provincial digital portals and payment gateways like eSewa or Khalti.
