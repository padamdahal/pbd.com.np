Frequently asked questions

Are these real Nepali names?

Yes. The lists are curated from authentic Nepali first names used across Nepal — both classic traditional names and more recent popular choices.

Does it include surnames?

No. This tool focuses only on first names (given names). You can pair any generated name with a surname of your choice.

Can I use these names for a baby?

Yes — many people use name generators for inspiration. Always check meanings, family traditions and any local naming customs (such as the Nwaran ceremony) before deciding.

What is the difference between traditional and modern?

Traditional names are classic, often Sanskrit-rooted names used for generations. Modern names are contemporary choices that have become popular in recent years in Nepal.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. All generation happens in your browser. Once the page has loaded you can use it without an internet connection. Your selections never leave your device.
