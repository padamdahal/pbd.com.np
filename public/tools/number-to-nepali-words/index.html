Number to Nepali words

Type a number in English or Nepali digits. Get the amount in words for cheques, bills and forms — लाख, करोड, अर्ब, खर्ब. Free and private. Conversion stays in your browser.

Formatted

In words / अक्षरमा

Tip: Copy sits under the result so you can paste into a cheque, Word file or message in one step.

How to use

  1. Type or paste a number. Commas are fine. Nepali digits work too.
  2. Words update as you type, grouped the Nepali way (pairs after the first three).
  3. Copy the words. Check them before using on a legal document.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a number to Nepali words?

Type any number in English or Nepali digits. The tool writes it using लाख, करोड, अर्ब and खर्ब.

Can I use Nepali digits like १२३?

Yes. English digits and Nepali digits both work, including decimals.

Is this useful for cheques in Nepal?

Yes. Amounts in words are common on cheques and official forms. Always double-check before you sign.
