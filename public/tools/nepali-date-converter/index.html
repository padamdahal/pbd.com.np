Frequently asked questions

How do I convert BS to AD?

Enter the Bikram Sambat year, month and day in the left panel. The matching Gregorian (AD) date appears immediately on the right. The tool uses verified calendar tables used for everyday official work in Nepal.

How do I convert AD to BS?

Enter the Gregorian year, month and day in the right panel. The corresponding Bikram Sambat date is shown on the left. Both directions work without any sign-up or download.

What BS years are supported?

The converter covers BS 2000–2090 (approximately AD 1943–2034). This range covers almost every birth certificate, citizenship paper and official document currently in use in Nepal.

Is Bikram Sambat the same as the Nepali calendar?

Yes. Bikram Sambat (BS / विक्रम संवत्) is the official calendar of Nepal. AD is the Gregorian calendar used internationally and in many computer systems.

Why is the Nepali year about 56–57 years ahead?

Bikram Sambat starts from 57 BCE. That is why a date in BS 2082 is roughly AD 2025–2026. The exact difference changes slightly depending on the month because Nepali months have varying lengths.

Can I use this for official or legal documents?

The converter is accurate for everyday use and follows standard published tables. For critical legal, immigration or court documents, always double-check the result against an official source or the issuing office.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. After the first load the page is cached by the site’s progressive web app, so you can convert dates even without an internet connection.

Do I need to install any software or app?

No. Everything runs in your browser. There is no sign-up, no app install and no data is uploaded to our servers.
