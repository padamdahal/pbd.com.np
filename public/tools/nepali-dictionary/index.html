    How to use the Nepali Dictionary

    1. Search in Devanagari or Romanized Nepali: Type Nepali Unicode (e.g., नेपाल) or phonetic English letters (e.g., nepal, kiran, gyan).
    2. Pick from autocomplete: Choose a matching headword from the suggestions dropdown or click a converted Devanagari candidate.
    3. Explore definitions and nearby words: View all numbered senses, grammar labels, etymology, and browse neighboring dictionary entries.

    Frequently asked questions

    What dictionary dataset does this Nepali Dictionary use?

    It uses an open structured dataset derived from the Nepali Brihat Sabdakosh (नेपाली बृहत् शब्दकोश), containing over 123,000 headwords with definitions, grammar labels, and etymology notes.

    Can I search using English / Romanized letters?

    Yes. If you do not have a Nepali keyboard enabled, type phonetically in English letters such as kiran, gyan, or swatantrata and select from the instant Devanagari candidates or press Enter.

    Does it work offline and keep my searches private?

    Completely. Your queries never leave your browser. Only static letter-range JSON chunks are fetched on demand and cached for fast repeat or offline lookups.

    What do abbreviations like ना., वि., क्रि., and [सं.] mean?

    They are traditional Nepali lexicography markers indicating part of speech (e.g., ना. for noun, वि. for adjective, क्रि. for verb) and language origin (e.g., [सं.] for Sanskrit).
