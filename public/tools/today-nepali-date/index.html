Frequently asked questions

What is today's Nepali date?

Today’s Nepali date is shown live in the card above in Bikram Sambat (BS). It updates according to Nepal Standard Time (UTC+5:45) and changes at midnight in Kathmandu.

What does “aaja kati gate” mean?

Aaja kati gate (आज कति गते) means “what is today’s date” in Nepali. It specifically asks for the current day of the month in the Bikram Sambat calendar.

Is Bikram Sambat the same as the Nepali calendar?

Yes. Bikram Sambat (BS / विक्रम संवत्) is the official calendar of Nepal. It runs roughly 56–57 years ahead of the Gregorian (AD) calendar.

When does the Nepali date change?

The Nepali (BS) date changes at midnight Kathmandu time (Nepal Standard Time, UTC+5:45). This page uses a live widget that follows that timezone.
