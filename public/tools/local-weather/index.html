Frequently asked questions

How does the local weather tool get my location?

It can use your browser’s location permission, or you can search for any city. Coordinates are only sent to the weather service to fetch the forecast and are not stored by PBD.

Is this weather data free?

Yes. The tool uses the free Open-Meteo API. No account or API key is required on your side.

Does it work outside Nepal?

Yes. Quick chips focus on Nepali cities, but the search works worldwide.

How often is the forecast updated?

Underlying models refresh regularly (often hourly). Search again or use “Near me” for the latest values.
