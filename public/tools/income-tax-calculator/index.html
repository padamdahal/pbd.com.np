Frequently asked questions

What are the income tax slabs for FY 2083/84?

1% on first NPR 1,000,000 (0% with SSF), 10% next 500k, 20% next 1M, 27% next 1.5M, 29% above 4M. Unified for all residents.

Is there still a separate married couple slab?

No. FY 2083/84 removed separate single/couple schedules.

Does SSF waive the 1% Social Security Tax?

Yes. SSF contributors get 0% on the first NPR 10 lakh.

Is this calculator official?

No. Independent estimate only. Verify with IRD Nepal or a tax adviser.
