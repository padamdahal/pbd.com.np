What are the income tax slabs for FY 2083/84?

1% on first NPR 1,000,000 (0% with SSF), 10% next 500k, 20% next 1M, 27% next 1.5M, 29% above 4M. Unified for all residents.

What is the Social Security Tax?

The first slab is often called Social Security Tax (1%). SSF contributors are exempt from this 1%.

Does SSF waive the 1% Social Security Tax?

Yes. SSF contributors get 0% on the first NPR 10 lakh.

Are retirement and insurance deductions allowed?

Yes. Retirement (SSF/PF/CIT) is capped at the lower of actual contribution, NPR 500,000, or one-third of assessable income. Life insurance up to NPR 40,000 and health insurance up to NPR 20,000 are typical limits used here.
