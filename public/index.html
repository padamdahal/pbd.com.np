Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using PBD tools.

  • Are these tools free to use?

    Yes. Every tool on Powered By Digital is free. There is no sign-up, no subscription and no paywall.

  • Do you store my data?

    No. Tools run in your browser (client-side). Your text, numbers and files are not uploaded to our servers for processing. Some tools may use local storage on your device for convenience (for example, game stats).

  • What is Bikram Sambat (BS) and can I convert it to AD?

    Bikram Sambat is the official calendar used in Nepal. Use the Nepali Date Converter to convert between BS and Gregorian (AD) dates, or open the interactive Nepali Calendar for month views and ISO copy.

  • Can I type in Nepali without a Nepali keyboard?

    Yes. The Nepali Typing tool converts Romanized English input to Nepali Unicode as you type. You can also convert Preeti font text with the Preeti ↔ Unicode converter.

  • Are the tax and bill calculators official?

    No. Income tax, vehicle tax and NEA bill calculators are estimates based on published slabs and rules. Always verify figures with IRD, transport offices or NEA before making payments or filing returns.

  • Do the tools work offline?

    After the first load, many tools continue to work offline via progressive web app (PWA) caching. A network connection is only needed for tools that fetch live data, such as weather or map tiles.
