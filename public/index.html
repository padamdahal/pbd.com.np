1. Nepali Language, Unicode Typing & Font Migration

Modern Nepali government portals, civil service forms (Lok Sewa Aayog), Nagarik App, and social media platforms require standard Devanagari Unicode. Many government offices and legacy archives still have documents typed in traditional Preeti or Kantipur fonts that appear garbled on mobile phones and modern computers.

  • Phonetic Unicode Typing: Type Romanized English (e.g., 'namaskar') to instantly produce clean Nepali Unicode text ('नमस्कार').
  • Preeti ↔ Unicode: Convert entire word processor files, notices, and legal briefs to Unicode or back without retyping.
  • Cheque Writing in Nepali: Convert numerical transaction amounts to formal Nepali words with Paisa for bank cheques and tax vouchers.
  • Spell Checker & Dictionary: Validate Devanagari spellings against stems from Madan Puraskar Pustakalaya and search meanings across 120,000+ words from the Brihat Sabdakosh.

2. Bikram Sambat (BS) Dates & Official Documents

Bikram Sambat is Nepal's official national calendar, running approximately 56.7 years ahead of the international Gregorian (AD) calendar. When applying for machine-readable e-Passports, foreign university admissions, visa processing, bank KYC, or government certificates, converting between BS and AD dates is a mandatory step.

  • BS ↔ AD Date Converter: Convert birth dates, marriage certificates, and land purchase dates between Bikram Sambat and Gregorian dates across BS 2000–2090.
  • Interactive Nepali Calendar: Browse monthly views, copy ISO dates with a single click, and drag dates directly into web forms.
  • BS Age Calculator: Calculate exact chronological age in completed years, months, and days from any BS date of birth, with countdowns to upcoming birthdays.

4. Land Measurement Systems: Hills (Pahad) vs. Terai

Nepal uses two distinct traditional land measurement systems alongside international metric units. Real estate transactions, land ownership certificates (Lalpurja), and municipal building permits in the hills use Ropani, while the Terai plains use Bigha. Converting between them requires precise standardized conversion factors.

  • Hill System (Ropani–Aana–Paisa–Daam): 1 Ropani = 16 Aana = 64 Paisa = 256 Daam = 5,476 square feet (508.74 m²). 1 Aana = 342.25 square feet.
  • Terai System (Bigha–Kattha–Dhur): 1 Bigha = 20 Kattha = 400 Dhur = 72,900 square feet (6,772.63 m²). 1 Kattha = 3,645 square feet.
  • Cross-System Conversion: 1 Bigha equals roughly 13.31 Ropani. Instantly convert any land area into Square Feet, Square Metres, Acres, or Hectares.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to everyday questions about using online tools in Nepal.

  • Are all tools on Powered By Digital free to use?

    Yes. Every tool on Powered By Digital is 100% free with no sign-up, no subscriptions, and no paywalls. All tools run directly inside your web browser without storing your inputs.

  • How do I convert Bikram Sambat (BS) birth dates to English (AD) dates for passports and forms?

    Use the Nepali Date Converter tool. Enter your date of birth in Bikram Sambat (BS) to instantly view the Gregorian (AD) date required for e-Passport applications, visa paperwork, academic admissions, and banking KYC. You can also explore month views and copy dates with the interactive Nepali Calendar.

  • How can I type in Nepali Unicode without installing software or keyboards?

    Open the Nepali Typing tool. Type phonetically in Romanized English (for example, typing 'nepal' automatically converts to 'नेपाल'). It runs in any web browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac, or Linux without installing special fonts or keyboard packages.

  • How do I convert Preeti font text to Unicode for government websites?

    Use the Preeti ↔ Unicode converter. Paste your legacy Preeti or Kantipur font text, and it converts instantly into Unicode Devanagari. Modern government portals such as Lok Sewa Aayog, the Public Service Commission, Nagarik App, and online exam portals strictly reject legacy fonts and require Unicode text.

  • How is personal income tax calculated in Nepal for FY 2083/84?

    Under the unified tax slabs published by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD), personal taxable income is taxed progressively: 1% on the first NPR 10,00,000 (waived to 0% for registered SSF contributors), 10% on the next 5 lakh, 20% on the next 10 lakh, 27% on the next 15 lakh, and 29% above 40 lakh. You can estimate your net take-home salary and allowable deductions with the Nepal Income Tax Calculator.

  • How does the NEA electricity bill calculator work with progressive slabs and VAT?

    The NEA Bill Calculator uses the approved residential tariff from the Electricity Regulatory Commission. It applies progressive unit energy rates and minimum monthly service charges based on your meter ampere (5A, 15A, 30A, or 60A). It also automatically computes the 5% VAT levied on the energy charge for consumption exceeding 50 units.

  • How many square feet are in 1 Ropani and 1 Bigha in Nepal?

    In the Hills, 1 Ropani equals 5,476 square feet (508.74 m²) and is divided into 16 Aana (342.25 sq ft each). In the Terai, 1 Bigha equals 72,900 square feet (6,772.63 m²) and contains 20 Kattha (3,645 sq ft each) or 400 Dhur (182.25 sq ft each). Use the Nepal Land Unit Converter for live cross-conversions.

  • How do I crop and compress photos for Nepal passports and DV lottery?

    The Image Compressor & Crop tool includes one-click presets for Nepal e-Passport (35 mm × 45 mm, ICAO standard) and the US DV Lottery (600 × 600 px square). Images are compressed and resized inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API without being sent over the internet.

  • Can I find all 77 districts and 753 local levels (palikas) on the Nepal map?

    Yes. The Nepal Map tool provides interactive layers for 7 provinces, 77 districts, and all 753 local levels (metropolitan, sub-metropolitan, municipalities, and gaunpalikas). You can search boundaries, inspect details, and upload custom CSV numbers to render color-coded choropleth maps.

  • Do PBD tools store my personal data or work offline?

    No. Calculations and conversions run entirely inside your browser. No files, dates, numbers, or text inputs are transmitted to or stored on our servers. In addition, the site's progressive web app (PWA) service worker caches resources so tools continue functioning offline after your first visit.
