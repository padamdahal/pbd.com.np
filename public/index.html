About PBD

Technology should solve problems, not create more of them.

PBD is a practical technology and digital information website focused on useful tools, understandable guides and information relevant to people in Nepal.

Instead of simply following every technology headline, we focus on information that remains useful: tools that solve everyday problems, guides that explain how things work and Nepal-specific information that can be difficult to find in one place.
