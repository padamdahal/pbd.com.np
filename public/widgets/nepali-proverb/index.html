How daily rotation works

The proverb is chosen from the collection using the current date in Nepal Standard Time (UTC+5:45). Everyone sees the same proverb on the same day.

Collection size

Over 120 traditional Nepali proverbs. We keep adding well-known, non-controversial ukhan that carry useful life lessons.

Free for any site

Personal blogs, news portals, school websites, local government pages — all welcome. No signup, no tracking inside the widget.

Works offline

Once loaded, the proverb data stays in the browser. The widget does not need a continuous connection to show today’s saying.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Nepali Proverb of the Day widget?

It is a free embeddable widget that shows one traditional Nepali proverb (उखान) each day, together with its English translation and a short meaning. You can place it on any website with a simple iframe.

How do I embed it on my website?

Choose your preferred options above (mode, language, theme, size) and click “Copy embed code”. Paste the iframe into your page’s HTML wherever you want the proverb to appear.

Does the proverb change every day?

Yes. In Daily mode the same proverb is shown to all visitors on a given day (Nepal Standard Time). Random mode picks a new one on every page load.

Are these proverbs copyrighted?

No. They are traditional Nepali folk proverbs passed down through generations and are in the public domain.

Can I request a specific proverb to be added?

Yes. Traditional, widely known, and non-controversial proverbs are welcome. Reach out via the About page.
