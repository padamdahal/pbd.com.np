About PBD

PBD is a small collection of free online utilities for everyday tasks in Nepal. The first tool is a Bikram Sambat ↔ Gregorian date converter; more tools such as Nepali typing, an EMI calculator and tax calculators are planned.

Everything runs in your browser. Results are provided for convenience; please verify dates and figures against official sources before using them in legal or financial documents.
