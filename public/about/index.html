About Powered By Digital

Powered By Digital (PBD) publishes free web tools and short guides so students, freelancers, office workers and small businesses in Nepal can finish common tasks without installing software or sharing personal data.

Our tagline is simple: Empowering people by digital means.

What we build

Client-side utilities for Nepali language (typing, dates, spelling), finance estimates (tax, electricity bills), maps, and everyday content tools. Most tools work offline after the first load.

Privacy

Tools run in your browser. We do not require an account. See our Privacy page for details.
