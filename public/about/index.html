About Powered By Digital

PBD — Empowering people by digital means.

Powered By Digital (PBD) builds free, simple online utilities so people in Nepal can get practical work done without friction. Our focus is everyday needs: dates, typing, age, weather, and more tools on the way.

Current tools: Nepali typing (Roman to Unicode), a Bikram Sambat ↔ Gregorian date converter, a BS age calculator, today’s Nepali date, and local weather. An EMI calculator and tax calculators are planned.

Everything runs in your browser. Results are provided for convenience; please verify dates and figures against official sources before using them in legal or financial documents.
